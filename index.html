Step 2: Perspective Projection Measurement

Given a known camera-to-object distance Z, the pinhole projection equations invert to recover real-world (X, Y):

u = fx · (X / Z) + cx  →  X = (u − cx) · Z / fx
v = fy · (Y / Z) + cy  →  Y = (v − cy) · Z / fy
